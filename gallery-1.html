Looking for food

This honey badger is looking for food.

It finds food mostly by smell.

Can you see the light gray stripe on its back?

Look at its front feet. Can you see the long claws? Honey badgers use them to dig fast. This photo was taken in a dry, dusty place, which is a common honey badger home.
