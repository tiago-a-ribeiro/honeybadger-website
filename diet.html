What Does It Eat?

Its name says honey, but honey is just a treat. A honey badger eats almost anything!

⬇️Download this image to add to your own project!

On the menu

  • 🐭Mice and rats: its favorite food! Learn about rodents.
  • 🐍Snakes: even ones with venomVenom: A harmful liquid that some animals, like some snakes, use when they bite or sting.! Learn about snakes.
  • 🦎Lizards and turtles: learn about lizards and tortoises.
  • 🦂Bugs: beetles, termites, and scorpions.
  • 🍯Bee nests: it eats the baby bees and the honey.
  • 🫐Plants: berries, fruit, and roots.

How often does it eat?

A honey badger looks for food every day. This is called foragingForage: To look around for food..

It eats lots of small meals, one small animal at a time. One big snake can be enough food for a whole day!

Did you know?

A honey badger can sometimes get better after a snake bite that would hurt other animals much more. Scientists think its body can fight some snake venom.

Learn more: a hunter that digs

A honey badger is a carnivoreCarnivore: An animal that eats mostly meat. most of the time. Its main way to hunt is digging. It walks with its nose close to the ground and sniffs for small animals hiding in holes.

When it smells preyPrey: An animal that other animals hunt for food., it digs very fast with its long front claws. It can dig dozens of holes in one day! Many of the animals it catches are mice, gerbils, and other rodents that hide underground.

Honey badgers do not waste much. They often eat almost all of a small animal, and their strong teeth can crunch hard food like turtle shells.

Learn more: getting water

Many honey badgers live in dry places where it does not rain much. They drink water when they find it, like the honey badger in our gallery.

When there is no water around, honey badgers can get much of the water they need from the food they eat. Juicy fruits, roots, and small animals all have water inside them.
