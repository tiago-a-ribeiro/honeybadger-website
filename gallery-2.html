Head up high

This honey badger lifts its head to look around.

Its eyes and ears are small. Its nose does most of the work.

Its black and gray fur says: "Leave me alone!"

Many animals with black and white colors, like skunks, use those colors as a warning. The bright stripe is easy for other animals to see, even at night.
