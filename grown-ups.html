For Parents and Teachers

Honey Badger HQ teaches children ages 6 and up about one wild animal. It covers where it lives, what it eats, its body, its babies, and how it behaves. Each page has short, simple text first, then "Learn more" sections for older readers.

Privacy

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, forms, or uploads.
  • We do not use cookies, trackers, or ads.

School projects

Every picture has a download button, and every page ends with a source children can copy. The Project helper page explains how to plan a project, use their own words, and cite sources.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to Simple English Wikipedia for more facts. These links have a ↗ mark so children know they are leaving this site.
