Its science name is Mellivora capensis.

The honey badger is small, but it is very brave. It digs, it climbs, and it is not scared of much!

Why are honey badgers famous?

Honey badgers are famous for being fearless. They are not very big, but they will stand up to animals much bigger than they are, like lions and leopards.

They are also very good at digging, and they are clever problem solvers. Scientists who study them say they are curious and stubborn. When a honey badger wants something, it keeps trying until it gets it!

Even though they are tough, honey badgers are a mammalMammal: An animal with fur or hair that drinks milk from its mom when it is a baby. You are a mammal too! just like dogs, cats, and you. They have fur, and their babies drink milk from their moms.
