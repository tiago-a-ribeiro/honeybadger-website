What Is Its Name?

Scientists give every animal a special science name. Let's learn the honey badger's!

⬇️Download this image to add to your own project!

Its science name

The honey badger's science name is Mellivora capensis.

Say it like this: mel-LIV-or-uh cap-EN-sis.

It means "honey-eater from the Cape". The Cape is a place at the bottom of Africa.

Some people also call it a ratel.

Did you know?

There is only one kind of honey badger in the whole world. It has no close cousins that look like it!

Its animal family

Its cousins are otters, wolverines, and weasels. They all have long bodies and short legs. Animals in this family are called mustelidsMustelid: An animal in the weasel family. Otters, weasels, wolverines, and honey badgers are all mustelids..

How scientists sort it

Scientists sort animals into groups, from big groups to small groups. This is called classificationClassification: Sorting animals into groups by how they are alike..

  • 🐾Animal (Animalia)
  • 🦴Has a backbone (Chordata)
  • 🍼MammalMammal: An animal with fur or hair that drinks milk from its mom when it is a baby. You are a mammal too!: has fur and drinks mom's milk (Mammalia)
  • 🥩Meat-eater group (Carnivora)
  • 🦦Weasel family (Mustelidae)
  • 🍯Honey-eater group (Mellivora)
  • 🦡Honey badger (Mellivora capensis)

Different kinds

Honey badgers in different places look a little different. Scientists call these sub-speciesSub-species: A group inside a species that looks a little different, often because it lives in a different place.. There may be up to 12 of them!

For older readers: see the sub-species
  • M. c. capensis (southern Africa)
  • M. c. abyssinica (Ethiopia)
  • M. c. indica (India)
  • M. c. inaurita (Nepal and nearby)
  • M. c. pumilio (southern Arabia)
  • M. c. wilsoni (Iran and Iraq)
  • M. c. buechneri (Turkmenistan)
  • M. c. leuconota, M. c. concisa, M. c. cottoni, M. c. maxwelli, and M. c. signata (parts of Africa)

Scientists do not all agree on this list. The honey badger was first named in 1776.

Learn more: why science names matter

People around the world speak many languages, so the honey badger has many common names. In some places it is called a ratel. Science names are the same in every country, so scientists always know which animal they mean.

Every science name has two parts. The first part is the genus, a small group of close relatives. The second part is the species. Together, Mellivora capensis names exactly one kind of animal.

The honey badger is in the meat-eater group called Carnivora. Cats, dogs, bears, and seals are in this group too! Animals in it are called carnivoresCarnivore: An animal that eats mostly meat., even though some of them, like the honey badger, eat plants sometimes.
