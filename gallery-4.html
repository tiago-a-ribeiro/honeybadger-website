A drink at night

This honey badger is drinking from a stream at night.

A hidden camera took this photo in India.

Scientists use hidden cameras so they do not scare the animals.

These cameras are called camera traps. They take a photo when an animal walks by. Camera traps help scientists learn where shy animals live, even if the animals only come out at night.
