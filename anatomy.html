How Big Is It?

A honey badger is small, strong, and built for digging. Let's compare it to you!

How tall?

It is about 25 centimeters tall at the shoulder. That is about as tall as a cereal box.

How heavy?

Dads weigh about 9 to 16 kilograms. Moms are smaller. A mom weighs about as much as 1 bowling ball.

How long?

From nose to bottom, a honey badger is about as long as a grown-up's arm. Its tail is about as long as a ruler.

Super body parts

  • 🛡️Thick, loose skin: It is tough like a rubber coat. It is so loose the badger can turn around inside it!
  • ⛏️Big claws: They are great for digging tunnels fast.
  • 👂Tiny ears: They are just little bumps. This may help keep dirt out when it digs.
  • 🦨Stinky smell: Like a skunk, it can make a very bad smell when it is scared.
  • ⚫Black and gray fur: These colors tell other animals: "Stay away!"

How does it breathe?

A honey badger breathes air, just like you!

Air goes in through its nose and into its lungs.

A strong muscle under the lungs, called the diaphragm, helps pull the air in.

Its big chest holds lots of air. That helps it dig for a long time.

Think about it

Put your hand on your belly and take a big breath. Can you feel your diaphragm move?

How long does it live?

In a zoo, a honey badger can live to about 24 years old. That is older than a high school student!

Life in the wild is harder, so wild honey badgers usually do not live as long.
