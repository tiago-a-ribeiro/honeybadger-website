    Stop 1: Pick your project

    What are you making?

    Tap one to get tips!

    Poster tips: Use a big title and big pictures. Put 2 or 3 short facts under each heading. Make it easy to read from across the room!

    Slides tips: Make one slide for each topic. Put one picture and 2 or 3 short facts on each slide. Do not put too many words on one slide.

    Report tips: Write a beginning, a middle, and an end. In the middle, write one paragraph for each topic.

    Stuck? Try a sentence starter:

    • The honey badger is a mammal that lives in…
    • One interesting fact about honey badgers is…
    • I was surprised to learn that…
    • In conclusion, honey badgers are…
