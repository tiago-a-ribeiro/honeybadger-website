Honey Badger Babies

Honey badger moms take very good care of their babies.

⬇️Download this image to add to your own project!

A new baby

A baby honey badger is called a cubCub: A baby honey badger. Baby bears, lions, and foxes are called cubs too..

The mom takes care of the cub all by herself.

She gets a cozy burrowBurrow: A tunnel or hole in the ground where an animal lives. ready before the cub is born. She lines it with soft grass.

How long does the baby grow inside mom?

Scientists call this time gestationGestation: The time a baby grows inside its mom before it is born..

How many babies?

  • 🦡1 cubMost of the time, a mom has just one cub.
  • 🦡🦡Sometimes 2Once in a while, she has two. More is rare.

Growing up

A new cub cannot see, and it has almost no fur.

Every few days, the mom carries her cub to a new burrow in her mouth. This keeps it safe.

When the cub is bigger, it follows mom. She shows it where to dig and what to eat.

The cub stays with mom for more than a year. That is from one birthday to the next, and a bit more!

Did you know?

By the time it leaves mom, the young honey badger is almost as big as she is!

Think about it

What is something a grown-up taught you, like a honey badger mom teaches her cub?

🔎 A busy mom

A honey badger mom has a lot of work to do. She has to find food for herself, feed her cub milk, and keep it safe, all on her own.

When she goes out to find food, she leaves the young cub hidden in the burrow. Moving the cub to a new burrow often makes it harder for other animals to find it.

Because she spends so long raising each cub, a honey badger mom does not have many babies in her life. That is why every cub is so important!

🔎 Learning to be a honey badger

Young honey badgers are not born knowing everything. They learn by watching their mom and copying her.

The cub learns which smells mean food, how to dig quickly, and how to stay safe. By the time it leaves, it is ready to live on its own.
