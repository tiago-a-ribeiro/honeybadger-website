🔎 Looking like a scientist

When scientists look at a photo of an animal, they ask questions. Where was it taken? What is the animal doing? What time of day is it?

Try it with these photos! Look for clues like dry grass, mud, or darkness. Each clue tells you something about how honey badgers live.

Every photo page has a download button. You can use these photos in your project, as long as you write down who took the photo.
