How Does It Act?

Honey badgers are brave, clever, and very stubborn!

⬇️Download this image to add to your own project!

How it finds food

  • 👃Sniffing: It smells animals hiding under the ground.
  • ⛏️Digging: It digs them out fast with its big claws.
  • 🐝Opening bee nests: Its thick skin keeps most bee stings out.
  • 🦅Hungry helpers: Some birds follow honey badgers. They catch the little animals that run away from the digging!

Myth or fact?

Some people say a bird called the honeyguide leads honey badgers to bee nests. It is a fun story, but scientists have not seen it happen. So it might be a myth!

How it stays safe

Lions and leopards are much bigger than honey badgers. But even lions often give up and walk away! Here is how a honey badger stays safe from a predatorPredator: An animal that hunts other animals for food.:

  • 📢Loud noises: It growls and rattles to sound scary.
  • 🦨Stinky smell: It makes a smell so bad that others back off.
  • 🛡️Tough skin: Its thick, loose skin is hard to bite through.
  • 💪Never gives up: It stands its ground, even against big animals.

Remember: Honey badgers are wild animals. If you ever see one, stay far away with a grown-up.

When does it sleep?

Honey badgers sleep in burrows, rock cracks, or hollow logs.

When it is cool, they may be awake in the day. When it is hot, they sleep all day and wake up at night. Animals that are awake at night are called nocturnalNocturnal: Awake at night and asleep in the day..

Honey badgers like to live alone.

A clever animal

Honey badgers are very smart. In zoos, some have learned to open gates and stack things to climb out!

🔎 How honey badgers talk

Honey badgers spend most of their time alone, but they still send messages to each other.

Their main way to send messages is with scentScent: A smell. Many animals leave scents to send messages to each other.. They rub their smell on rocks, trees, and the ground near their burrows. It is a bit like leaving a note that says, "I was here!"

They also make sounds. An upset honey badger can growl, hiss, and make a loud rattling noise. Cubs make small whining sounds to call their moms.

🔎 The famous escape artist

One honey badger named Stoffel lived at an animal rescue center in South Africa. He became famous for escaping again and again.

The people who cared for him said he opened gates, climbed walls, and even used things like sticks, rocks, and rakes to help him climb out. Every time they fixed one problem, Stoffel found a new way out!

Stories like this show how good honey badgers are at solving problems. Scientists are still learning how smart they really are.
