Words to Know

Found a big word? Look it up here!

Burrow
A tunnel or hole in the ground where an animal lives.
Classification
Sorting animals into groups by how they are alike.
Diaphragm
A strong muscle under the lungs. It helps you breathe in.
Forage
To look around for food.
Habitat
The place where an animal lives.
Mammal
An animal with fur or hair that drinks milk from its mom when it is a baby. You are a mammal too!
Mustelid
An animal in the weasel family. Otters, weasels, wolverines, and honey badgers are all mustelids.
Nocturnal
Awake at night and asleep in the day.
Predator
An animal that hunts other animals for food.
Prey
An animal that other animals hunt for food.
Species
One kind of animal. All honey badgers are one species.
Sub-species
A group inside a species that looks a little different, often because it lives in a different place.
Venom
A harmful liquid that some animals, like some snakes, use when they bite or sting.
